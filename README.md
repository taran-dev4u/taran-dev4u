### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software and AI/ML Engineer** pursuing my **M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering at University at Buffalo, SUNY** (graduating Dec 2025). Previously, I engineered backend services and reporting pipelines at **iVinGo Solutions**, conducted geospatial climate AI research at **NRSC, Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)**, and contributed production-grade code to major open-source ecosystems including **NVIDIA NeMo**, **FlexMeasures**, and **AquaScope**.

- 🔭 **Primary Focus:** High-throughput backend systems, distributed data infrastructure, production ML/VLM pipelines, and resilient cloud architectures.
- 🔬 **Research & Publications:** Co-authored peer-reviewed research on atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$ geospatial modeling published in the **International Journal of Remote Sensing (IJRS)**, [DOI: 10.1080/01431161.2025.2562005](https://doi.org/10.1080/01431161.2025.2562005).
- 🌐 **Open-Source:** Active contributor to enterprise and scientific open-source platforms across PyTorch, Hugging Face, Flask, FastAPI, Pandas, and GeoJSON.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Distributed systems, VLM/LLM fine-tuning, Extreme Value Theory, API design, database indexing, kernel scheduling, and end-to-end data pipelines.
